YoloMCL 1.1.1 fixes cape rendering in the wardrobe.

## Fixes

- Show the decorated side of a cape on the outside, matching its thumbnail. Capes no longer display their lining in place of the main artwork.
- Correct horizontal orientation and texture mapping on the side, top and bottom edges.
- Preserve fine details in high-resolution cape textures by scaling texture coordinates before sampling.

## Validation and performance

- Add pixel-level regression tests for all six cape faces at 64×32, 128×64 and 256×128, including texture origins, transparency and buffer reuse.
- Retain zero heap allocations per frame during steady rotation. Local rendering benchmarks show no increase in frame time.

## Updating

Existing official installations can update from Settings. Updates remain optional.

For manual installation, download the archive matching your operating system and processor: `.zip` for Windows, or `.tar.gz` for macOS and Linux. Extract the complete archive before launching. `amd64` is Intel/AMD x64; `arm64` includes Apple Silicon.
